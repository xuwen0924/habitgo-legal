Frequently Asked Questions

What is Habitgo?

Habitgo is a lightweight tool that helps you build habits and track your growth. We want to make every small act of persistence clearly visible, so change can happen naturally.

Who is Habitgo for?

Whether you want to wake up early, exercise, read, drink water, or learn a new skill, Habitgo lets you create a habit plan that works for you.

How do I start building a habit?

Create a habit, set its frequency, target count, and reminder time, then check in as planned. We recommend starting with one simple, small goal.

What habit schedules are supported?

Habitgo supports daily schedules, selected weekdays, a certain number of days per week, a certain number of days per month, and more.

Will missing a check-in affect my records?

An occasional interruption does not mean failure. You can restart at any time. Habitgo focuses more on long-term progress than a perfect streak.

Can I view habit statistics?

Yes. Habitgo uses monthly and yearly views and completion trends to help you understand how consistently you follow your habits and how you are growing.

Does Habitgo support reminders?

Yes. You can set a reminder time for each habit. Please make sure notifications for Habitgo are enabled in your system settings.

Will I lose my habit data?

We recommend using the data backup feature regularly to save your habits and check-in records. Confirm carefully before clearing data, as deleted data may not be recoverable.

Is Habitgo free?

Habitgo provides basic features for free. Upgrading to a membership unlocks more habits, themes, icons, and advanced features.

How do I restore my membership after changing devices?

Use the same Apple ID or Google account you used for the purchase, then select “Restore Purchases” on the membership page.

Why does Habitgo emphasize “starting small”?

Lasting change usually does not come from a burst of motivation, but from one small step you can complete every day. Start by taking action, then let consistency become a habit.

What is Habitgo’s philosophy?

Every check-in is not about proving perfection. It is a way of telling yourself: today, I moved one step closer to the person I want to become.
